💪 What is Project Rock?

Started in 2016 by Dwayne “The Rock” Johnson in partnership with Under Armour, Project Rock is more than just workout gear—it’s a lifestyle brand built around Johnson’s values: resilience, discipline, and relentless effort. The brand identity—including the iconic bull logo and black‑and‑yellow color palette—echoes themes of strength and perseverance.

Key Launch Elements

GBOAT (“Greatest Bag of All Time”): A limited-edition gym bag that sold out instantly.

Rock Clock: A motivational alarm clock that delivers daily reminders to “chase greatness.”

Major Apparel & Footwear Lines

  • “Through The Work” (2020): UA Charged Cotton apparel and PR2 shoes with HOVR technology.
  • Football-Inspired (2021): Designed around Johnson’s football journey.
  • Black Adam Collaboration (2022): Elite training line with PR5 shoe and Black Adam gear.

Behind the Branding

Droga5 helped shape the visual identity—emphasizing raw toughness and motivational grit to match Johnson’s persona.

Related Media & Business Ventures

Apparel is sold globally via Under Armour, UA Brand Houses, and retailers like Dick’s Sporting Goods.

Seven Bucks Productions (founded by Johnson and Dany Garcia) produces his film and TV projects, grossing over $4.6 billion.
